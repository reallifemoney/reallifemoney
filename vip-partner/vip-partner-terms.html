VIP Partner terms

This is written in plain English on purpose. If anything's unclear, just ask - the short version is: come to the workshop, share it honestly, and we'll pay you fairly for what it brings in.

£

How and when you get paid

You're paid at the end of the month in which the relevant workshop takes place - so if someone books using your code for a workshop on the 12th, that payment lands in your end-of-month payout, not straight away. Your dashboard will always show what's confirmed and what's still pending.

✕

Cancellations

If someone uses your code to book but then cancels, or doesn't attend, no payment is made for that person. Payouts are for people who actually attend a workshop, not just for a booking being made.

#

You must disclose it's an ad

Any content you post about the workshop - Stories, Reels, posts, anything - needs to include #ad. This isn't optional; it's the law, and it protects you as much as it protects us.

🔒

The workshop content belongs to Real Life Money

Everything taught in the workshop - the slides, the frameworks, the explanations, the materials - belongs to Real Life Money. You're welcome to film your own reactions and experience of attending, but you can't copy, reproduce, or teach the workshop content itself elsewhere.

🔒

Your code is yours to share, not to sell

Your discount code is for sharing with your own audience. It shouldn't be sold, listed on discount/voucher sites, or handed off to anyone else to promote on your behalf.

≈

No guaranteed earnings

How much you earn depends entirely on how many people book and attend using your code - there's no minimum or guaranteed amount. We'll always be upfront with you about how the numbers are tracking.

⚠

Staying in the programme

This is an invite-only programme built on trust. If a partner breaks these terms - for example, not disclosing an ad, or reselling a code - we reserve the right to remove them from the programme and withhold any unpaid balance for the period involved.

📝

Changes to these terms

If these terms ever change, we'll let you know directly before they apply to you - you won't be held to a change you weren't told about.

Any questions at all - just message directly, always happy to talk it through.
