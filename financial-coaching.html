Frequently asked questions

What can we talk about?

We can explore credit, mortgages, investing, pensions, tax and other questions connected to your financial goals. Tell me what you need help with and we can discuss whether coaching is right for you.

How much does coaching cost?

Financial coaching costs £50 per hour. I’ll discuss your needs and the next steps with you before arranging a session.

Is this regulated financial advice?

No. I offer education and guidance to help you understand your options, not personal recommendations for financial products or regulated mortgage advice. If you need regulated advice or specialist tax advice, you should speak to an appropriately qualified professional.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Just bring your questions and goals. I’ll let you know if it would be useful to have any documents to hand; please don’t put sensitive details in the enquiry form.
