Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is this suitable for complete beginners?

Absolutely. It’s designed for people who feel like they know nothing, and it’s still useful if you’ve dabbled before. No prior knowledge needed.

Do I have to drink alcohol?

Not at all. The pub is just a relaxed setting, and soft drinks and hot drinks are just as welcome. Drinks are bought separately at the bar.

How much does it cost?

£50 per person for two evenings of 90 minutes each, in a small group of no more than 12. No upsells and nothing to buy afterwards.

Will you tell me what to invest in?

No. This is financial education, not financial advice. I’ll give you the knowledge and tools to make your own decisions confidently, but I won’t recommend specific products.

Is investing risky? Could I lose money?

Yes, all investing carries risk, and the value of investments can fall as well as rise. Risk is something you can understand and plan around, and that’s a big part of the workshop.

What if I can’t make the second session?

Just let me know and I’ll move you to the second session of another workshop.

Who are you and why should I trust you?

I’m Leo, a secondary school maths teacher and former financial adviser. I’m not selling products and there are no referral fees or follow-up sales calls, just two evenings of honest financial education.
