Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience to join?

None at all — this course is built for complete beginners. If you've never opened an investment account before, you're exactly who this is for.

How is the online version different from the in-person workshop?

Same content, same live teaching from me, just over Zoom instead of in a room in Bristol. It's a smaller group so there's still plenty of room for questions.

What if I can't make one of the live sessions?

Every session is recorded and sent to you afterwards, so you won't miss anything even if you have to drop off partway through.

Will you tell me exactly what to invest in?

I'll teach you how to make that decision confidently yourself — the principles behind fund selection, risk, and account types — rather than handing you a stock tip. This is financial education, not regulated advice.

How do I pay, and when?

Once you reserve your place you'll get a secure Stripe payment link. You can pay straight away, or let me know if you'd like a few days — your seat is fully secured once payment lands.

What do I need on the night?

Just a laptop or phone with Zoom, a notepad, and about 90 minutes of your evening. A Zoom link is sent ahead of each session.
