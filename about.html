Hi, I'm Leo

A former financial adviser turned maths teacher, on a mission to make investing make sense for everyone.

My story

Where I started

I've always loved numbers, and I've always loved working with people. I studied Maths with Business at university, and financial advice looked like the perfect way to bring it all together. I qualified as a financial adviser and worked in the industry, helping people plan for their futures.

Why I left

The longer I stayed, the more I felt that it wasn't reaching the people who needed help most, and it wasn't fulfilling me either. So much of it is wrapped in jargon that keeps ordinary people out. Helping people has always been my passion, so I made a big decision: I left to become a teacher.

Teaching, and Real Life Money

Teaching felt right straight away. I teach maths by day, and through Real Life Money I teach investing in plain English: no jargon, no pressure and no selling. My workshops are education, not financial advice, so I'll never push you towards a particular product. I just want you to leave feeling confident and ready to take the first step.

Away from the classroom

I live in Bristol with my wife and our cat, Paddy. I come from a big family, so there's rarely a quiet get-together, and when there's cricket on, you'll find me watching.
